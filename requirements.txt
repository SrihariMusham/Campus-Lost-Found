Lost & Found Portal - Runtime Requirements

Node.js: 20.x or newer
npm: 10.x or newer
MySQL: 8.0 or newer
Modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari
Internet connection: required for email notifications and CDN-hosted frontend assets

Node dependencies are managed through package.json and package-lock.json.
Database setup is provided in database/schema.sql.
Environment variables are documented in .env.example.
